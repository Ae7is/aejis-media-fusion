Terms, Revisions & Refund Policy

Aejis Media Fusion — last updated [date]

1. What we do

We produce ready-to-post AI short videos (vertical, about 60–90 seconds) for creators on a monthly plan. Each plan includes prompt writing, AI image and clip generation, voiceover, captions and editing.

2. Plans and billing

  • Plans are billed monthly, in advance. Your plan (Starter, Growth or Pro) sets the number of shorts per day, as shown on our website.
  • Work begins once payment is confirmed and your intake form and reference material are received.
  • Prices are shown in USD. Nigerian clients can pay in naira at the rate shown on the payment link.
  • We may change prices with at least [14] days’ notice. The price you signed up at stays the same until your next renewal.

3. What we need from you

  • A completed intake form, and a reference image or video of the style you want.
  • Topics or script ideas, or permission for us to choose them.
  • You confirm that you own or have the right to use everything you send us. We won’t knowingly recreate copyrighted characters, brands or real people’s likenesses, and we may decline requests that do.

4. Delivery

  • Shorts are delivered in weekly batches through a shared Drive folder (7, 14 or 21 shorts, depending on your plan).
  • Your first batch is delivered within 7 days of payment and onboarding. After that, batches arrive on the same weekday each week.
  • Posting to your accounts is optional and free. We’ll never ask for your passwords. If you want us to post, we’ll use manager or collaborator access, or you can post the files yourself.

5. Revisions

  • Each short includes 1 revision if requested within 48 hours of delivery.
  • A revision covers changes to the edit, captions, voiceover or visuals within the original concept. A completely new concept counts as a new short.
  • Extra revisions and rush delivery are available as paid add-ons.

6. About AI-generated content

AI tools can produce unexpected or inconsistent results. We review every short before delivery and regenerate clips that don’t meet our standard, but we can’t guarantee that outputs will be identical to your reference, that a character will look the same across every video, or that any platform will approve or promote your content.

7. Ownership and usage

  • Once you’ve paid in full for a batch, you may use the delivered videos on your own channels and accounts.
  • Our tools have their own terms of use, which apply to content created with them.
  • We may show your videos in our portfolio only if you agreed on the intake form. You can withdraw that permission at any time.

8. Cancellation and refunds

  • You can cancel any time before your next billing date. Your plan runs until the end of the period you’ve paid for.
  • Because production begins as soon as a batch is started, we don’t refund a batch once production has begun.
  • If we fail to deliver a batch you’ve paid for, we’ll either complete it within [7] days or refund the undelivered shorts.
  • Pausing a plan for one month is possible if you tell us before your billing date.

9. Communication

We reply to messages on WhatsApp and email within [24 hours] on business days.

10. Liability

We’re not responsible for lost revenue, followers or platform actions linked to your content. Our total liability for any problem is limited to the amount you paid in the month it occurred.
